Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what data the AI Video Factory application ("the application", "we") receives through Google OAuth and how it is used. The application is an internal tool used only by the AI Video Factory studio owner to manage the studio's own YouTube channels.

Data we receive from Google

When a channel is connected, the application requests the following Google OAuth scopes for that YouTube channel:

  • youtube.upload – to upload our own videos;
  • youtube.readonly – to identify the connected channel (channel ID and name) and read our own video status;
  • youtube.force-ssl – to update the title, description, thumbnail and privacy status of videos we uploaded;
  • yt-analytics.readonly – to read analytics for our own channels.

We also receive OAuth access and refresh tokens that allow the application to perform these actions.

How we use the data

The data is used only to publish and manage the studio's own videos on the studio's own channels and to measure their performance. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share data received from Google with any third party. The data is not transferred to anyone except as required by law.

AI Video Factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest on the studio's own computer and are never published or sent to other services. Access to that computer is limited to the studio owner. We keep tokens only as long as a channel stays connected.

How to revoke access

  1. Open myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access).
  2. Select AI Video Factory and choose Remove access.

After access is revoked, the application can no longer upload to or read data from that channel. You can also ask us to delete any stored tokens by email; we will delete them promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
