AI Video Factory

AI Video Factory is an independent media studio. We produce short documentary videos on science, space, history, technology and nature, and publish them on our own YouTube channels.

What the application does

The AI Video Factory application is an internal tool used only by the studio owner. It helps us assemble our own videos from our own narration and properly licensed or public-domain footage, and then upload them to YouTube channels that we own and manage.

When the owner connects a channel, the application uses Google OAuth to request access to that channel so it can:

  • upload our own videos to that channel (uploads start as private);
  • update the title, description, thumbnail and visibility of videos we uploaded;
  • read basic channel information and analytics for our own channels.

The application is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's channels.
